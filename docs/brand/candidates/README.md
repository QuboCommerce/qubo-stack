# Qubo glyph candidates

Six first-round candidates for the Qubo mark. Palette: black, white and one accent, Hot magenta `#FF2E88` (deeper `#E0136F`) or Cyan `#00C2E0` (deeper `#0097B5`). Every container is a true superellipse (n between 2.5 and 5), generated in `.scratch/brand/` (`geom.mjs`, `glyphs.mjs`, `build.mjs`). Each glyph comes as `NN-name.svg` (magenta), `-cyan.svg` and `-mono.svg` (`currentColor`). The app icons (`-app.svg`) for 01, 03 and 05 are full bleed on `#111111`, with the mark inside the central 80% safe zone.

**01 Notch.** A squircle q drawn with two radii: the open side is soft, the stem side is one straight edge with tight corners. The descender is a detached square accent module on that same straight edge, a block clicked into place under the letter. Carries: modular, adjusted, objective-driven. Strengths: reads as "q" at 16px, the straight stem edge keeps it from reading as "9", and the wider gap keeps the module separate at 16px. Strong app icon. Risk: the tab is small, so at 16px it reads as a dot more than a block.

**02 Layout.** Three squircle tiles in a sidebar and two-block layout, the accent block turned a few degrees as it is being dropped in. Carries: modular, customizable but not overwhelming (three blocks, not nine), site builder. Strengths: the most legible at 16px, explains the product at a glance. Risk: tile grids are a crowded category, and nothing in it says "Q".

**03 Pivot.** One module (squircle bowl plus stem) used twice: upright it is the q, and an exact 180 degree turn of the same path gives the b, the two letters that bracket "qubo". The two halves sit a narrow gap apart, stem to stem, so they lock into one monogram. Carries: modular, customizable, adjusted. Strengths: a real idea behind the shape, perfectly rotationally symmetric, works as an app icon and beside the wordmark. Risk: wide format, the narrow gap closes up in single colour at 16px, and it reads as "qb" until people learn the story.

**04 Aim.** A squircle ring Q whose bottom-right corner is chamfered, with the tail as a small accent module landing on that flat face. Carries: objective-driven, accessible, safe to the touch (soft ring). Strengths: a clear capital Q, calm and open. Risk: chamfer plus diamond is the only hard angle in the set, and at 16px the tail shrinks to a dot (still reads as Q).

**05 Reach.** A handheld squircle split by the arc of a thumb's reach; the corner inside the arc is the accent zone. One of the two marks with a same-hue tonal shift (accent to deep accent). Carries: handheld, ergonomic, safe to the touch, freedom after onboarding (everything you need sits within reach). Strengths: the most ownable silhouette, already squircle-shaped and fits the planned UI, holds up in single colour because the arc gap carries the idea. Risk: abstract, no letter, and the gradient is lost in mono (by design).

**06 Folio.** A fine monoline wordmark drawn as paths with soft squircle bowls. The q and b are one drawn form turned 180 degrees (the 03 idea, set in type), the u keeps its own right stem, and the accent full stop shares the bowls' exponent and tucks in close under the o's shoulder. Carries: accessible, adjusted, objective-driven (a sentence that ends). Strengths: reads plainly as "qubo" from 32px up, quiet and editorial, pairs with any of the marks. Risk: the detail is subtle, and as a fine wordmark it is not for 16px (pair it with 01, 03 or 05 at favicon size).
