Design language shell. Themes gain a brand kit (logos, mark, favicon, sharing image, voice), surface gradients, word decor presets (colour, underline, squiggle, brush stroke, box, circle, marker, gradient fill), page transition presets, menu enter and exit motion, and ambient effects (snow, particles, aurora, grain) with a seasonal schedule. Every section gains a background gradient, an effect and art motion (float, parallax, spin), plus arch and torn edges and two more entrance animations. Blocks get `f.decor`, `f.duration`, `f.easing` and `f.preset` fields. Heading highlights become decor marks picked per language; existing highlights keep working.
