Files in the inbox. Visitors can attach photos and PDFs in the live chat, files sent by e-mail are imported into the conversation, and staff can attach files to replies by clicking the paperclip, pasting or dropping them. E-mailed replies carry their files. Files stay private to your team and the customer they belong to. Chat files are removed after 30 days unless you keep them, and any image or PDF can be saved to the media library.
