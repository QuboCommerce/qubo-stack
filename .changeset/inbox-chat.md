Live chat: a new "Live chat" block (`ChatLauncher`) lets visitors chat from any page. Messages open chat conversations in the inbox, staff replies arrive live over SSE, returning visitors see an unread badge, signed-in customers are recognised, and replies are e-mailed when the visitor has left.
