Inbox: snooze a conversation (1 hour to a week) and it comes back unread on time, with its own Snoozed tab. The list flags customers who have waited over 4 hours (amber) or a day (red). New Settings → Inbox page shows whether e-mail in and out is set up and lets owners rename forms and choose who is notified of submissions.
