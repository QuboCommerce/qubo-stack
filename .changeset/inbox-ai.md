AI for the inbox, on your own AI account. Under Settings > AI, pick OpenAI, Anthropic, Mistral, xAI, OpenRouter or your own model server, paste a key and set a monthly token budget. New messages get a short summary, a category, a mood and an urgency within seconds, urgent ones move up, and spam is flagged. In any conversation, "Draft with AI" writes a reply in the customer's language using the thread, their recent orders, matching products and your house rules; nothing is sent until you press Send. Included from the Starter plan.
