The inbox is split into Everything, Forms, E-mail, Chats and Tickets, as tabs and as sidebar entries, each with its own open count. A thread is drawn for where it came from: a form post is a card with labelled fields, links to call or mail, the page and language it was sent from, and buttons to reply, add a note or make a ticket; an e-mail reads like a letter with its To and Cc lines and the quoted trail folded away; a chat stays in bubbles. Any conversation can become a numbered ticket, per site, from the header, the form card or the `/ticket` command. The reply box is a rich text editor (bold, italic, links, lists, quotes, pasted files), and Enter sends in chats. E-mail replies show the customer as a locked To and keep the thread's Cc and Bcc as chips that only go away with their own button. Typing `/` offers note, reply, ticket, waiting, resolve, snooze and assign to me. Cc addresses from incoming mail join the thread automatically.
