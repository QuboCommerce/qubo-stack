Media modifiers. Images and videos can take a shape (circle, arch, soft square, blob, slanted edge or your own SVG or PNG), reveal on scroll (fade, rise, wipe, grow) and a subtle or strong parallax. Images also get hover effects (zoom, lift, grey to colour, shine) and a caption over the picture. Looping videos play like a GIF only while on screen and stay on the poster when reduced motion is on. Cards get a hover choice: lift, zoom image or highlight border.
