Site switcher mega menu and in-app provisioning. The topbar switcher now shows every organisation you belong to with theme-painted site cards, unread badges, plan usage and lock states, and grows from a list into a grid as sites are added. "New site" sets up a complete site (pages, header, footer, theme, contact form) from a type preset and lands you in it; "New organisation" creates a legal entity you own. Both respect the plan's site and organisation ceilings. The disabled "Create site" button on Settings → Sites now opens the same dialog.
