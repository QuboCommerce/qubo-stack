SteelReveal, a scroll-driven hero drawn in CSS.

A two-door steel cabinet, pinned while the visitor scrolls, whose doors
swing open on `animation-timeline: view()` to reveal the headline, text
and buttons inside, with an optional photo as the interior. Scroll length,
cabinet height, handles, hint label and interior color scheme are fields.
Browsers without scroll timelines, reduced motion and the editor canvas
get the open state. Sticky site headers now publish `--qb-header-h` so
pinned sections sit below them.
