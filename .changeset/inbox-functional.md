The inbox works like an app on both phone and desktop: list, thread and a contact panel fill the screen without page scroll, and the panel becomes a sheet on smaller screens. Right-click (or long-press) any conversation for read or unread, status, snooze, priority, assignment, ticket, copy and spam; select several to do the same in bulk. Filter by unread, high priority or assignee, sort four ways, and search message text and ticket numbers. Each message has copy, quote in reply, copy link and "mark unread from here", which brings back a "New" line. Form posts get per-field copy, copy all, call and map. The reply box stays compact until used, keeps drafts per conversation and can send and resolve in one go. Keyboard shortcuts cover the whole inbox (`?` lists them).
