Media library. Upload images, videos and PDFs once per organisation and use them on any of its sites: keep a file to one site or share it with all of them. QuickPick opens from the new Media page, from every image and video field in the Studio, and from the product page, where you can now add, reorder and remove product images. Drop or paste files to upload them. Files are stored on the server's disk by default, or in any S3-compatible bucket.
