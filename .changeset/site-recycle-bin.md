Draft sites can be deleted from the Sites page. A deleted site goes to a recycle bin at the bottom of that page, stops resolving on every host and no longer counts against the plan; it can be restored or deleted for good at any time, and is purged automatically after 60 days. "Move" is now "Transfer".
