Page settings and a live canvas. Every page can pick its own transition (played when a visitor leaves it) and its own ambient effect, or turn either off, or follow the theme. Preview links now show the theme draft, so theme changes can be checked before publishing. The Studio canvas runs effects, header menus and looping videos while you edit, and a toolbar button plays the page transition over the canvas.
