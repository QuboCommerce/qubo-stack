Studio collaboration. One person edits a page at a time, and everyone else watches the canvas change live. Followers can ask for control, and the editor gets a "Give control / Keep" prompt. Control passes straight away when the editor is you in another tab, has been idle for a minute, or has left. Click anyone's avatar to watch them: you follow their page, the field they are in and the block they selected until you press Stop or Esc.
