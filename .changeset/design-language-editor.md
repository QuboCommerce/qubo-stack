Design language editor. The theme panel gains a "Design language" group: Brand (logos, mark, favicon, sharing image, voice), Gradients, Word highlights, Effects (site-wide effect with a date window) and Transitions (page transition presets with a Test button, plus menu and sheet motion). Block fields get a duration slider with an exact value, an easing picker, preset pickers with swatches and a "Highlight words" dialog that picks the marked words in every language. A shared `@qubo/blocks/runtime` runs transitions and effects for both the studio previews and the storefront.
